1The decision

Which two-tag concept the creative director of a hypothetical 5-person Bangkok indie studio pitches at the greenlight on Mon 2 Nov 2026; it fixes the next 18-month project's genre, scope and Steam-page tags. Had no pair passed the white-space test, the pitch would be a fast follower in a proven niche.

2Why a network

A tag-count spreadsheet ranks popular tags; it cannot see whether two tags sit in separate audience neighbourhoods, i.e. whether combining them bridges a structural hole. That needs neighbours and paths. Prior Steam-tag networks (Li & Zhang, 2020) were descriptive; this one drives a build decision.

8What would change my mind
