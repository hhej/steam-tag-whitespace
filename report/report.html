Which tag pair should an indie game use? First-Person × Multiplayer

MADT 7202 Mid-Term · Panjapol Ampornratana 6810424009 · Repo: github.com/hhej/steam-tag-whitespace

Steam is the largest PC game store. Players vote tags onto each game; the top tags drive store search.

1The decision

An indie studio's creative director picks the next game's two lead tags; if no pair passed the selection rule, they would pick a crowded one (table, last row).

2Why a network

Counts show how common a pair is and how well it sells, not whether its tags belong to separate groups or which tag connects them. That needs the network.

3Data

CheckS1 and S2 agree: 87% overlap in top-5 tags; review counts correlate at 0.98.
BiasFavours games that sold; reviews count buyers, not demand; late-2024 games had less time.

4Graph construction

NodeA Steam tag. Undirected, weighted tag–tag projection of the game–tag bipartite graph.
EdgeCo-occurrence in a game's top 5 tags; suggests relatedness, not a stated relation.
ResolutionCo-op, Online/Local Co-Op and Local Multiplayer merged into Multiplayer (84% of Horror × Multiplayer games were also tagged co-op).
WeightNPMI: how much more often two tags meet than chance (0 = chance). Raw counts put 9 of the top 10 edges on the 5 most common tags; NPMI puts none.
WindowTop 5 tags per game, as the store page shows; with all 20, #1's review advantage drops from 5.1× to 3.0×.
Excluded“Indie”; Early Access, Free to Play, adult tags; on >25% of games: Singleplayer, Adventure, Action, Casual, 2D, 3D, Atmospheric, Exploration.
PruningTag on ≥1.5% of games (136); edge needs NPMI ≥ 0.10 and ≥0.5% of games (45) with both tags. 439 tags → 80 nodes, 177 edges.

5The picture

Selection rule: of pairs on ≥73 games, keep those not crowded (crowding = games ÷ expected ≤ 1.5; expected = games with A × games with B ÷ 9,094) whose tags share ≤20% of neighbours; rank by median reviews.

6Findings

  • #1 is about as common as chance (NPMI 0.02, so no edge), yet its median game gets 5.1× the reviews (permutation test, p < 0.001). Horror games overall get only 1.0×.
  • Survival Horror is the only tag connecting #1's two groups of neighbours: the one tag to watch.
  • Mostly robust: top at all 8 pruning settings but in only 59 of 100 bootstrap resamples; on the 2025 test set, still not crowded (1.14×) with 2.4× reviews.

7Recommended action

  • Week 0: pitch a first-person multiplayer game.
  • Weeks 1–6: prototype. Week 7: Coming Soon page tagged First-Person and Multiplayer/Online Co-Op.
  • Every quarter: re-run and check #1 and Survival Horror (section 8).

8What would change my mind

First-Person is spreading (27% of 2025 games). If #1 passes 1.5× crowding, I switch to #2 while it stays below 1.5× (1.45× in 2025), then to #3.
