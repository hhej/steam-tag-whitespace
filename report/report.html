Which tag pair should an indie game use? First-Person × Multiplayer

MADT 7202 Mid-Term · Panjapol Ampornratana 6810424009 · Repo: github.com/hhej/steam-tag-whitespace

Steam is the largest PC game store. Players vote tags onto each game; the top tags drive store search.

1The decision

An indie studio's creative director chooses the two tags for the next game. If no pair passed the selection rule (section 5), they would take a proven but crowded pair such as Building × Simulation.

2Why a network

Counts can't show whether two tags attract different players. The network can: tags with few shared neighbours point to audiences that rarely meet, which one game may reach together.

3Data

SourceS1 = FronkonGames dataset (Hugging Face); S2 = live Steam store + reviews.
ObtainedS1 download (Sep 2025 snapshot). S2: 1,000 random S1 games (random seed 7202), scraped 2026-10-14.
LegalS1 MIT licence. S2: robots.txt allows /app/, /appreviews/; 1 req/1.5 s. No personal data.
PeriodReleased Jan 2023 – Dec 2024.
VolumeS1: all 9,094 Indie games with ≥10 reviews. S2: 996 with a live page.
CheckS1 vs S2: mean top-5 tag Jaccard 0.87; Spearman ρ of reviews 0.98.
BiasFavours games that sold; reviews count buyers, not unmet demand; late-2024 games had less time.

4Graph construction

NodeA Steam tag. Undirected, weighted tag–tag projection of the game–tag bipartite graph.
EdgeCo-occurrence in a game's top 5 tags; suggests relatedness, not a stated relation.
ResolutionCo-op, Online/Local Co-Op, Local Multiplayer merged into Multiplayer: 84% of Horror × Multiplayer games were also co-op, so splitting them hid crowding.
WeightNPMI: co-occurrence vs chance, −1 to 1 (0 = chance). By raw count, 9 of the top-10 edges touch the 5 commonest tags; by NPMI, 0.
WindowTop 5, as shown on the store page. With all 20, the pair covers 375 games (mostly minor tags) and its reviews drop from 5.1× to 3.0×.
Excluded“Indie” (genre filtered on); release-model, adult tags; >25% of games: Singleplayer, Adventure, Action, Casual, 2D, 3D, Atmospheric, Exploration.
PruningTag on ≥1.5% of games (136); edge: NPMI ≥ 0.10, ≥0.5% shared (45). 439 tags → 80 nodes, 177 edges.

5The picture

Selection rule: among pairs on ≥73 games (0.8%), keep those not crowded (games ≤ 1.5× chance, where chance = games with A × games with B ÷ 9,094) whose tags sit in separate neighbourhoods (≤20% shared neighbours); pick the one whose median reviews are highest relative to the median game (40).

6Findings

  • First-Person × Multiplayer is near chance (NPMI 0.02, no edge), yet its median game gets 5.1× the reviews (permutation p < 0.001); 68% are horror, led by Lethal Company.
  • 8 of the 10 highest-review pairs are crowded (>1.5× chance), e.g. Building × Simulation (2.54×).
  • Robust: #1 at all 8 edge cutoffs; above the median game in 100/100 bootstraps. Weak spot: #1 in 59/100 (top 3 in 74), near the 73-game minimum.

7Recommended action

  • Week 0: pitch a first-person multiplayer horror game; plan B is Horror × Multiplayer.
  • Weeks 1–6: prototype. Week 7: Coming Soon page, tagged First-Person and Multiplayer first.
  • Before full production: re-run on 2025 releases; switch if crowding passes 1.5× or the pair leaves the top 3.

8What would change my mind

"Not crowded" is against chance, not the 2023–24 co-op horror boom. If 2025 releases push it past 1.5×, I switch to the #3 pair, Pixel Graphics × RPG.
