Which tag pair should an indie game use? First-Person × Multiplayer

MADT 7202 Mid-Term · Panjapol Ampornratana 6810424009 · Repo: github.com/hhej/steam-tag-whitespace

Steam is the largest PC game store. Players vote tags onto each game; the top tags drive store search.

1The decision

An indie studio's creative director picks the next game's two lead tags; if no pair passed the selection rule, they would pick a crowded one (table, last row).

2Why a network

Counts give crowding and reviews. Which uncrowded pairs join separate neighbourhoods, and through which tag, needs neighbours and paths, which counts lack.

3Data

SourceS1 FronkonGames steam-games-dataset (Hugging Face); S2 live Steam store.
ObtainedS1 download (Sep 2025). S2: 1,000 random S1 games (random seed 7202), scraped 2026-10-14.
LegalS1 MIT licence. S2: robots.txt allows /app/, /appreviews/; 1 req/1.5 s; no personal data.
VolumeS1: 9,094 Indie games, 2023–24, ≥10 reviews; hold-out: 2,076 from Jan–Jun 2025. S2: 996 live pages.
CheckS1 vs S2: top-5 tag Jaccard 0.87; reviews Spearman ρ 0.98.
BiasFavours games that sold; reviews = buyers, not unmet demand; late-2024 games had less time.

4Graph construction

NodeA Steam tag. Undirected, weighted tag–tag projection of the game–tag bipartite graph.
EdgeCo-occurrence in a game's top 5 tags; suggests relatedness, not a stated relation.
ResolutionCo-op, Online/Local Co-Op, Local Multiplayer → Multiplayer (84% of Horror × Multiplayer games were co-op too).
WeightNPMI: co-occurrence vs chance (0 = chance). By raw count, 9 of the top-10 edges touch the 5 commonest tags; by NPMI, 0.
WindowTop 5 tags per game, as the store page shows; with all 20, #1's reviews drop from 5.1× to 3.0×.
Excluded“Indie”; Early Access, Free to Play, adult tags; on >25% of games: Singleplayer, Adventure, Action, Casual, 2D, 3D, Atmospheric, Exploration.
PruningTag on ≥1.5% of games (136); edge needs NPMI ≥ 0.10 and ≥0.5% of games (45) with both tags. 439 tags → 80 nodes, 177 edges.

5The picture

Selection rule: of pairs on ≥73 games (0.8%), keep those not crowded (games ≤ 1.5× chance; chance = games with A × games with B ÷ 9,094) with ≤20% shared neighbours; rank by median reviews ÷ 40.

6Findings

  • #1 is near chance (NPMI 0.02, no edge) yet gets 5.1× the median reviews (p < 0.001, permutation test). Not horror: all horror games get 1.0×, #1's non-horror games 11.5×.
  • Survival Horror is the only link between #1's two neighbourhoods (figure): the one tag to watch.
  • Mostly holds: #1 at all 8 edge cutoffs; above the median in 100/100 bootstraps (#1 in 59); 3.35× without its top 5 games; passes the 2025 check.

8What would change my mind

First-Person is spreading (27% of 2025 games). If #1 passes 1.5× crowding, I switch to #2 while it stays under 1.5× (1.45× in 2025); if both are crowded, to #3.
