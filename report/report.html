Which Steam tag pair should an indie studio build? Horror × Multiplayer

MADT 7202 Mid-Term · Panjapol Ampornratana 6810424009 · Repo: github.com/hhej/steam-tag-whitespace

Steam is the largest PC game store. Players vote tags onto each game; the top tags drive store search.

1The decision

An indie studio's creative director picks the two-tag concept for the next game. With no pair passing the rule in section 5, they would take a proven, crowded pair, e.g. Management × Simulation.

2Why a network

Counts can't tell whether two tags attract different players. The network can: tags sharing few neighbours draw on audiences that rarely meet, so a game combining them can reach both.

3Data

SourceS1 = FronkonGames dataset (Hugging Face); S2 = live Steam store + reviews.
ObtainedS1 download (Sep 2025 snapshot). S2 scraped 2026-10-14: random 1,000 (seed 7202).
LegalS1 MIT licence. S2: robots.txt allows /app/, /appreviews/; 1 req/1.5 s. No personal data.
PeriodReleased Jan 2023 – Dec 2024.
VolumeS1: all 9,094 games with ≥10 reviews. S2: 996.
CheckS1 vs S2: top-5 tags Jaccard 0.87; reviews ρ 0.98.
BiasFavours games that sold; reviews count buyers, not unmet demand; late-2024 games had less time.

4Graph construction

NodeA Steam tag. Undirected, weighted tag–tag projection of the game–tag bipartite graph.
EdgeCo-occurrence in a game's top 5 tags; suggests relatedness, not a stated relation.
ResolutionNone: Steam tags come from a fixed list.
WeightNPMI: co-occurrence vs chance, −1 to 1 (0 = chance). By raw count, 9 of the top-10 edges touch the 5 commonest tags; by NPMI, 0.
WindowTop 5, as the store page shows. With all 20, the pair's reviews vs the median game fall from 3.1× to 2.5× (200 games, mostly minor tags).
Excluded“Indie” (seed); release-model, adult tags; tags on >25% of games: Singleplayer, Adventure, Action, Casual, 2D, 3D, Atmospheric, Exploration.
PruningFlat cutoffs: tag on ≥1.5% of games (136); edge NPMI ≥ 0.10 and ≥0.5% shared (45); pair ≥0.8% (73).
Size439 tags → 81 nodes, 177 edges.

5The picture

Selection rule: among pairs on ≥73 games, keep those not crowded (games ≤ 1.5× chance, where chance = games with A × games with B ÷ 9,094) whose tags sit in separate neighbourhoods (≤20% shared neighbours); pick the one whose median reviews are highest relative to the median game (40).

6Findings

  • Horror × Multiplayer is used about as often as chance (NPMI 0.07, no edge), so the niche isn't crowded, yet its median game gets 3.1× the typical game's reviews (p < 0.001).
  • 9 of the 10 highest-review pairs are crowded (>1.5× chance), e.g. Management × Simulation (3.8×).
  • Robust: #1 at all 8 edge cutoffs; beats the typical game in 100/100 resamples and on S2. Weak spot: #1 in only 36/100 resamples, as it sits right at the 73-game minimum.

7Recommended action

  • Week 0: pitch Horror × Multiplayer at greenlight; plan B is Pixel Graphics × RPG.
  • Weeks 1–6: prototype. Week 7: Coming Soon page with Horror, Multiplayer as first tags.
  • Before full production: re-run on 2025 releases; plan B if over 1.5× crowded or out of the top 3.

8What would change my mind

At 1.37× chance the pair is near the 1.5× crowding limit (crossed in 17/100 resamples). If 2025 releases cross it, I switch to plan B.
