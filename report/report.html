Which tag pair should an indie game use? First-Person × Multiplayer

MADT 7202 Mid-Term · Panjapol Ampornratana 6810424009 · Repo: github.com/hhej/steam-tag-whitespace

Steam is the largest PC game store. Developers set a game's tags, players vote on them; top tags drive search.

1The decision

An indie studio's creative director picks the next game's two lead tags; if no pair passed the selection rule (section 5), they would pick a crowded pair (table).

2Why a network

Counts show how common a pair is and how well it sells, not whether its tags belong to separate groups or which tag connects them. That needs the network.

4Graph construction

NodeA Steam tag. Undirected, weighted tag–tag projection of the game–tag bipartite graph.
EdgeCo-occurrence in a game's top 5 tags; suggests relatedness, not a stated relation.
ResolutionCo-op, Online/Local Co-Op and Local Multiplayer merged into Multiplayer (84% of Horror × Multiplayer games were also tagged co-op).
WeightNPMI (co-occurrence above expected). Raw counts give 9 of the top 10 edges to the 5 most common tags; NPMI gives none.
WindowTop 5 tags per game, as the store shows; with all 20, #1 falls from 4.3× to 2.7× positive reviews.
Excluded“Indie”, Early Access, Free to Play, adult; on >25% of games: Singleplayer, Adventure, Action, Casual, 2D, 3D, Atmospheric, Exploration.
PruningTag on ≥1.5% of games (136); edge needs NPMI ≥ 0.10 and ≥0.5% of games (45) with both tags. 439 tags → 80 nodes, 177 edges.

5The picture

Expected = games with both tags if picked independently (A's games × B's games ÷ 9,094); crowding = games ÷ expected; positive reviews = thumbs-up reviews of the pair's median game. Rule: of pairs on ≥73 games (0.8%, for stable medians), keep crowding ≤ 1.5 and ≤20% of combined neighbours shared; rank by positive reviews.

6Findings

  • #1: 78 games vs 71 expected (NPMI 0.02, under the 0.10 edge cutoff), yet 4.3× typical positive reviews (permutation test, p < 0.001); horror games overall 1.0× typical. An association, not cause; reach, not rating (76% positive).
  • Survival Horror is the only tag linking #1's two neighbour groups; if it spreads, the gap closes.
  • Narrow lead: top at all 8 pruning settings (4 NPMI cutoffs × 2 minimums) but in 60 of 100 resamples; 2025 games: crowding 1.14, 2.2× positive reviews.

7Recommended action

  • Week 0: pitch the game.
  • Weeks 1–6: prototype; playtest target 87% positive.
  • Week 7: Coming Soon page with both tags.
  • Quarterly: re-check crowding.

8What would change my mind

If #1's crowding passes 1.5 or Survival Horror's share of releases rises, I switch to #2 (1.45 in 2025), then #3; if playtests miss 87%, to #3 (88% positive).
