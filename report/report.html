Puzzle × Story Rich: the white space in Steam's indie tag network

MADT 7202 Mid-Term · Panjapol Ampornratana 6810424009 · Repo: github.com/hhej/steam-tag-whitespace

1The decision

Which two-tag concept the creative director of a hypothetical 5-person Bangkok indie studio pitches at the greenlight on Mon 2 Nov 2026; it fixes the next 18-month project's genre, scope and Steam-page tags. Had no pair passed the white-space test, the pitch would be a fast follower in a proven niche.

2Why a network

A tag-count spreadsheet ranks popular tags; it cannot see whether two tags sit in separate audience neighbourhoods, i.e. whether combining them bridges a structural hole. That needs neighbours and paths. Prior Steam-tag networks (Li & Zhang, 2020) were descriptive; this one drives a build decision.

4Graph construction

Node, edgeA tag. An edge asserts both tags are among the same game's top-5 tags by votes.
ExcludedSeed “Indie”; tags on >25% of games (Singleplayer, Adventure, Casual, Action, 2D, 3D, Atmospheric, Exploration, Simulation, First-Person); release-model and adult tags.
WeightNPMI, not raw count. By raw count 9 of the top-10 edges touch the 5 commonest tags (top: Action–Adventure, 66 games); by NPMI, 0.
WindowTop-5 tags, not all 20: the store shows the top tags. With all 20, 72 games carry the winning pair (mostly as minor tags) and its lift falls from 5.9× to 1.9×.
PruningTag on ≥15 games; NPMI ≥ 0.10 and ≥5 shared games. 391 tags → 98 nodes, 249 edges.

5The picture · size = games with tag · colour = median reviews · edge width = NPMI

6Findings

  • Puzzle is the main broker (betweenness 0.17, constraint 0.13), linking 14 tags across separate neighbourhoods.
  • White space: Puzzle × Story Rich. Only 9 games combine them (chance expects 11) and their neighbourhoods barely overlap (0.10), yet their median game has 302 reviews, 5.9× the cohort's 51 (permutation p = 0.021); 22% are top-decile hits vs 10% overall.
  • Crowded vs open. The top-lift pair, Management × Strategy (13.0×), is 2.4× over-supplied. Ours is #1 at all 8 cutoffs and on S1 tags; lift > 1 in 99/100 bootstraps (90% CI 1.6–39×), but #1 in only 3/100: with n = 9, its rank is fragile.

7Recommended action

  • Mon 2 Nov 2026: creative director pitches Puzzle × Story Rich at greenlight; plan B is the fast follower Management × Strategy.
  • 2 Nov – 11 Dec: 6-week vertical slice. Mon 14 Dec: Steam “Coming Soon” page live with Puzzle and Story Rich as its first two tags.
  • Fri 15 Jan 2027, go/no-go: ≥1,500 wishlists in 30 days → full production; else plan B and re-run on 2025 releases.

8What would change my mind

Co-occurrence is not causation, and 9 games is a thin base one breakout could carry. If the wishlist test misses or the pair loses its lead on 2025 releases, I switch to plan B (Management × Strategy).
