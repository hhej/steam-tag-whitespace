Which Steam tag pair should an indie studio build next? Horror × Multiplayer

MADT 7202 Mid-Term · Panjapol Ampornratana 6810424009 · Repo: github.com/hhej/steam-tag-whitespace

Steam is the largest PC game store. Players vote tags onto each game; the top tags drive store search.

1The decision

The creative director of an indie studio decides which two-tag game concept to build next. If no pair had passed the tests below, they would pick a proven but crowded pair such as Management × Simulation.

2Why a network

Tag counts show which tags are common. They cannot show which two tags sit in separate parts of the tag network yet do well when combined; that needs each tag's neighbours.

3Data

SourceS1 FronkonGames dataset (Hugging Face). S2 live Steam store + review API.
ObtainedS1 download (Sep 2025 snapshot). S2 scraped 2026-10-14: random 1,000 (seed 7202), 1 req / 1.5 s.
LegalS1 MIT licence. robots.txt allows /app/, /appreviews/. No personal data.
PeriodReleased Jan 2023 – Dec 2024.
VolumeS1: all 9,094 games with ≥10 reviews (network). S2: 996 (check).
Cross-checkS1 vs S2 top-5 tags: Jaccard 0.87; reviews ρ = 0.98.
BiasThe review floor favours games that sold; reviews count buyers, not unmet demand; late-2024 games had less time.

4Graph construction

NodeA Steam tag (one node type). Undirected, weighted tag–tag projection of the game–tag bipartite graph.
EdgeCo-occurrence in a game's top 5 tags; suggests relatedness, not a stated relation.
ResolutionNone needed: Steam tags are a fixed list, already resolved by the platform.
WeightNPMI (0 = what chance predicts). With raw counts, 9 of the top-10 edges involve the 5 most common tags; with NPMI, 0.
WindowTop 5, as on the store page. With all 20, 200 games carry the pair, mostly as minor tags; lift falls from 3.1× to 2.5×.
Excluded“Indie” (seed); release-model and adult tags; tags on >25% of games: Singleplayer, Adventure, Action, Casual, 2D, 3D, Atmospheric, Exploration.
PruningFlat cutoffs: tag on ≥1.5% of games (136); edge NPMI ≥ 0.10 and ≥0.5% shared (45); pairs scored if ≥0.8% (73).
Size439 tags → 81 nodes, 177 edges.

6Findings

  • Horror × Multiplayer is combined about as often as chance (NPMI 0.07, so no edge), and the tags share 8% of their neighbours. Yet its median game gets 3.1× the cohort's 40 reviews (p < 0.001).
  • 9 of the 10 highest-lift pairs are crowded, e.g. Management × Simulation (3.8× chance).
  • #1 at all 8 NPMI cutoffs. Lift > 1 in 100/100 bootstraps (90% CI 1.9–7.8×) and on S2 (1.6×). #1 in 36/100; in 42 it dips under the 73-game floor.

7Recommended action

  • Week 0: pitch Horror × Multiplayer at greenlight; plan B is Pixel Graphics × RPG.
  • Weeks 1–6: prototype. Week 7: Coming Soon page with Horror, Multiplayer as first tags.
  • Before full production: re-run on 2025 releases; plan B if over 1.5× crowded or out of the top 3.

8What would change my mind

At 1.37× chance the pair is near the 1.5× crowding limit (crossed in 17/100 resamples). If 2025 releases cross it, I switch to plan B.
